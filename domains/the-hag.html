■ Overview

The Sea Witch

The Hag is one of the older awnsheghlien, a sea-witch transformed by Azrai-tainted bloodline into a creature half-woman, half-monstrosity, dwelling in salt-marsh and tidal creek along the western coast. Canon attests her appearance "in Kordan" centuries ago and her continuous magical harassment of Grabentod and other coastal Brecht realms ever since.

The Hag’s realm has no traditional capital city; it is a network of marsh-warrens, ruined fishing villages, and submerged caves, watched over by the Hag’s personal servants and the corrupted creatures who serve her. Her holdings are difficult to map and harder to garrison.

Her enemies are universal: every coastal realm has lost ships and crews to her storms, her sea-monsters, and the corruption she sends inland with the tide. Grabentod's pirate court — ironically — is one of her most determined adversaries, having lost more vessels to the Hag than to any rival pirate prince.

■ Persons of Interest

Key NPCs

The Hag

Sea-Hag of the Mire

An ancient awnsheghlien of great Azrai bloodline. Specific stats vary by edition; the Hag is consistently presented as a powerful magic-user-class awnshegh with sea and weather affinities. Her detailed write-up is in the Blood Enemies sourcebook. (Source: havens.txt:774-781; Blood Enemies stat block.)

■ Current Affairs

Political Situation

The Grabentod Feud

The Hag has hated Grabentod for generations; canon attests this hatred explicitly (havens.txt:781). The pirate court of Grabentod survives the Hag's magic only by extensive counter-magic and by the willingness of pirate-captains to accept routine catastrophic losses to her sendings.

Coastal Corruption

The Hag's curse spreads inland with the tide: villages near her marshes lose children to mysterious illness, fish-catches turn rotten, and crops blight where her servants have walked. The realm's reach is wider than its formal borders.

Solitary by Nature

No awnshegh has formally allied with the Hag. Her power is too tied to her geography — the salt-marsh, the tide, the coastal mist — to be exported, and her temperament is too capricious for sustained pact. She is a regional curse, dangerous but not strategic.
