■ Overview

Carved From Alamie
Held Ever Since

Tuornen is what remained when the western provinces of Alamie broke away — a forest duchy founded in fire, sustained in vigilance. Duchess Laela Flaertes inherits a realm whose every border tells the same story: the Duke of Alamie has never accepted that his western marches are gone, and every season of relative peace is, in truth, the lull between his next attempt and the last.

The forests of Tuornen are old and rich, and the magical sources that flow beneath them are among the strongest in Anuire. The wizard Fhylie the Sword keeps her tower in the duchy and tends those sources jealously — an arrangement Laela protects, because a Tuornen without potent sources is a Tuornen with one less reason for outsiders to hesitate.

Tuornen holds its independence through forest warfare, well-placed alliances with Mhoried to the north, and the sheer difficulty of campaigning across its wooded terrain. The duchess herself is no warrior of legend, but she is patient, exact, and deeply read in the affairs of her enemies. The Duke of Alamie has miscalculated her three times. He is unlikely to live long enough to do it a fourth.

■ Figures of Note

Key Characters

Duchess Laela Flaertes

Duchess of Tuornen · House Flaertes

Laela Flaertes inherited a duchy with one enemy older than its own borders. She is patient, methodical, and politically exact — the kind of regent who summons rivals to her court in Haes one month after a perceived insult and lets the silence do the work. Her Anduiran bloodline runs strong, and the Flaertes line has held Tuornen since the break from Alamie. Her doctrine is simple: hold what was won, repay every betrayal, and never let a wizard forget who owns the source he sits on.

Fighter Anduiras Bloodline Lawful Good

Fhylie the Sword

Source-Holder · The Mage of Tuornen

A female elven wizard whose source holdings span every province in the duchy. Fhylie has bound her fate to Tuornen, and the duchess to her: as long as the Flaertes throne stands, no rival magus enters the woods uncontested, and as long as Fhylie tends the ley lines, Tuornen's wizards have no cause to look elsewhere. The bargain is older than the duchess. It is also the single greatest thing keeping Tuornen on the map.

Wizard / Source-Holder Sidhelien
■ Threats & Alliances

Political Landscape

01

The Alamie Aggression

Alamie has never reconciled itself to the loss of its western provinces. The Duke of Alamie views Tuornen as a wound in his own realm, a legacy of the war his ancestors lost. Border skirmishes are a seasonal occurrence, full-scale war is never far from the horizon, and the duchess governs as if every winter peace is a lie told between rounds.

Conflict · Territory · War
02

The Mhoried Friendship

Mhoried to the north has long been Tuornen's most reliable neighbour. The Mhor and the duchess share a common enemy in the great houses east of the river, and the alliance — quiet, unsentimental, written in supplies more than treaties — has saved both realms in turn. It is the steady ground beneath Laela's policy.

Diplomacy · Elves · Balance
03

Forest Magic

Tuornen's rich magical sources are the envy of every wizard-regent in Anuire. The ley lines that run through its ancient forests are unusually potent, and Fhylie the Sword holds them all. Controlling — or corrupting — those sources is a prize worth going to war over, and outside mages know they cannot move without provoking both wizard and duchess at once.

■ Power Structure

Holdings & Assets

Sources

Strong

Tuornen's greatest asset. The ancient forests channel powerful magical ley lines, making source holdings among the richest in Anuire.

Law

Moderate

The Duke's law extends through settled areas but the deep forests remain largely ungoverned, following older elven customs and traditions.

Temples

Moderate

The Militant Order of Cuiraécen and the Orthodox Imperial Temple of Haelyn (under the Nichaleir line) are the dominant temple holdings, serving the settled populations in the capital and towns.

The Forests

Critical

The forests themselves are Tuornen's greatest strategic asset. They hamper invading armies, shelter guerrilla fighters, and channel magical power.
