Each direction is a complete visual personality, not a palette swap. Typography, hero composition, mood, and the role of red are all different. Read each rationale.md alongside its mockup before deciding — the constraints each direction takes on (and the risks each accepts) matter more than which one looks best in the iframe.

CSS-gradient placeholders stand in for the hero illustration. Production needs commissioned art in the chosen direction's mood — that decision flows out of which direction is picked, not into it.
