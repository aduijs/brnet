A Saga of Cerilia

In most D&D settings, you play a wandering adventurer.
In Birthright, you play a ruler.

The blood of the old gods does not forget. It runs through mortal veins and asks one thing of you: what will you do with the land that is now yours? The throne is not your reward. The throne is your campaign.
