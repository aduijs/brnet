Cerilia · The Birthright Setting · Est. 1995

In most D&D settings, you play a wandering adventurer.
In Birthright, you play a ruler.

The blood of the old gods flows through mortal veins. You inherit a province; its harvests, its borders, its enemies, and its dead are now yours to govern. The throne is the campaign.

02 · The Six Regions

Choose a Crown

Each region holds its own court, its own customs, and its own way of dying. Pick the one that calls.

Anuire

The fallen empire. Twelve duchies sharpening knives in the throne room's dust.

Rjurik

Long forests, long winters, long memory. Druidic law older than any crown.

Khinasi

Caravan-cities, scholar-kings, and the long open road between magic and trade.

Brechtür

City-states, guild fortunes, and a hundred ports that owe loyalty only to their ledgers.
